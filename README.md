# Find a table

A Bangalore restaurant recommender. You choose a locality, a budget, a cuisine, a minimum rating, and any extra preferences. The app filters real restaurant listings, then asks a language model to rank a short list and explain each pick.

The restaurant name, cuisine, rating, and cost always come from the catalog. The model only ranks and writes the explanation.

**Live site:** https://zomato-c9.vercel.app

## What you enter

- Locality, chosen from Bangalore areas in the catalog
- Budget: low, medium, or high
- Cuisine, optional
- Minimum rating, from 0 to 5
- Extra preferences, such as family-friendly or quick service

## What you get

A short list of restaurants. Each card shows the name, cuisines, rating, estimated cost for two, and a short explanation of why it fits.
